Role of the Silver Master Franchisee

  • Acts as the master franchisee for the allotted district.
  • Can establish any number of franchise outlets within the territory, subject to the applicable agreement.
  • Can grant new franchise opportunities within the district.
  • Must meet the minimum franchisee-start requirement applicable to the territory within one year.
  • New outlets must maintain the prescribed territory spacing: minimum 3 km in metro/mega cities and up to 5 km in smaller Tier 2/Tier 3 cities.

State Territory Rights

Gold Package

₹10,00,000
State-owned territory rights for specified smaller states

Gold Master Franchisee rights

  • Acts as the master franchisee at state ownership.
  • Can establish franchise outlets within the allotted state territory.
  • Can grant Silver Package sub-franchises within the territory.
  • For a Silver sub-franchise, the Gold master may share only its own franchise portion of brand fee and royalty; the organisation's portion remains untouched.
Illustrative fee-sharing structure

Where a new franchise generates a ₹50,000 brand-fee amount under the applicable structure, the master-franchise portion may be divided between the Gold master and its Silver sub-franchise, for example ₹25,000 + ₹25,000. Similarly, the Gold master's 2% royalty portion may be divided as 1% + 1%.

The organisation's applicable portion remains protected as specified in the master-franchise structure.
