Signature Menu

Crafted for maximum flavor and fried to a spectacular crunch right when you order. Delicious, sizzling hot, and made to satisfy your seafood cravings instantly.

Fish Fillet

Expertly marinated fish fillets coated in a golden, crispy breading. Flaky on the inside and packed with your choice of savory garlic or bold masala, it is the ultimate crunchy seafood snack.

Fish Fingers

Tender fish fillets cut into perfect bite-sized strips and coated in a golden, ultra-crispy crumb. Flaky on the inside with a satisfying crunch, they make for an irresistible, savory snack paired perfectly with your favorite dipping sauce.

Spicy Corn Fish

Succulent fish bites marinated in fiery spices and coated with a crunchy cornflake crust. Fried to a beautiful golden crisp, these tender, zesty delights deliver the ultimate punch of bold flavor and explosive texture in every single bite.

Prawn Torpedo

Sticking to its classic, elongated shape—our Prawn Torpedo delivers a neat, uniform crunch packed with succulent prawn flavor and finished with a fresh squeeze of lime.

Prawn Butterfly

Perfectly expanded and fried to golden perfection—our Prawn Butterfly features a wide, crispy crunch that holds maximum flavor, served with a sweet and spicy dipping sauce.

Fish Lolipop

Fun to hold and impossible to resist—our Fish Lollipops are crispy on the outside, packed with a savory fish filling on the inside, and served perfectly on a stick!

Tawa Fish Fry

Flavor-packed and cooked on the bone—our Tawa Fish Fry is deeply scored with signature spices for a rich, traditional taste that real fish lovers will adore.

Fish & Chips

Keep it classic with our traditional Fish & Chips—a perfectly sized, golden-battered fish fillet served hot alongside seasoned rustic potato wedges and creamy tartar sauce.

Special Combo

The ultimate seafood feast—our Special Combo is loaded with a generous variety of crispy fried fish, juicy prawns, and golden potato fries, served with traditional mushy peas and tartar sauce.

French Fries

Indulge in a plate of our classic golden fries, served hot and crisp with your favorite dip.

Spring Potato

Indulge in the perfect crunch with our freshly spiraled, golden-brown Spring Potatoes.

Tawa Fish Fry

Garnished with fresh herbs and seared to perfection—our Pomfret-style Tawa Fish Fry offers a delicate crunch and rich, bone-in flavor crafted for true seafood enthusiasts.
